# LSI SmartTrack AI Training Document Inventory

## Purpose

Use this inventory to collect and prepare approved LSI materials for the AI assistant. The assistant should use these sources to answer student questions consistently, explain English lessons, and guide users through LSI SmartTrack. Start with a small, trusted set and expand it as materials are approved.

> These documents are a knowledge base for retrieval and answering. They do not need to be used to train or fine-tune a model.

## Recommended first three documents

Begin with these three sources, if current approved copies are available:

1. **LSI Student Handbook** — student rules and regulations.
2. **FAQ Documents** — frequently asked questions and approved answers.
3. **User Manual** — how to use LSI SmartTrack.

Together, these provide policy, common answers, and product guidance. Add English learning and assessment materials when their owners have reviewed them.

## Intake checklist

For each source document:

- [ ] Obtain it from the responsible office or content owner.
- [ ] Confirm it is approved for student-facing use and identify who approved it.
- [ ] Confirm its effective date and whether it replaces an older version.
- [ ] Remove personal, confidential, or unnecessary identifying information.
- [ ] Check that text is readable and complete; include headings and tables where possible.
- [ ] Record the owner, source location, approval date, effective date, and review date.
- [ ] Mark the document ready only after the checks above are complete.

Do not treat an unapproved draft, expired policy, or document with unresolved conflicting instructions as an authoritative answer source.

## Maintenance

Review policy, handbook, assessment, and SmartTrack instructions when the responsible office publishes a change. Retire superseded copies from the AI knowledge base so outdated instructions are not retrieved. Keep a clear owner and review date for every approved source. When sources disagree, refer the conflict to the responsible office before using either as authoritative guidance.
